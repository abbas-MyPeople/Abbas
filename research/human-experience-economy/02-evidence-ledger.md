# Evidence ledger

Every number used elsewhere in this folder, with its source and a confidence grade. Research date:
**2026-07-28**. All web-sourced unless noted.

## Confidence grading

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **A** | Primary source — company filing/earnings release, government statistical agency, peer-reviewed, or the org itself |
| **B** | Reputable secondary — established trade press or major outlet reporting a primary source |
| **C** | Secondary with unclear methodology — industry blog, vendor content marketing, consultancy summary, survey with unpublished method |
| **D** | Weakly sourced — SEO/affiliate content, promotional blog, unverifiable claim. **Directionally interesting, do not quote.** |

**Discipline for this folder:** nothing graded C or D gets asserted as fact in analysis without the
qualifier attached. Several widely-circulated figures in this space are D-grade and get repeated as
if they were A-grade; a few are flagged explicitly below.
